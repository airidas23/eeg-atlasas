Changes in anatomy-data.js: triangle decimation to 18,500 per hemisphere,
coordinate quantization to 0.01 mm and binary-to-JavaScript encoding.
The display translates and uniformly scales these surfaces for the HydroCel
montage. This display alignment is illustrative. The BigBrain anatomy is
independent of the MODMA participants and EEG recording geometry.
